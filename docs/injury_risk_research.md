## What this means for the design
- **Previous injury is the strongest, most consistent factor** (2, 3) -> main driver of the score.
- **Turf is NOT a general risk factor** in soccer (4, 5, 6). The user's "turf streak raises risk" idea is kept only where evidence points: ankle/foot (5, 6), and as a labelled *design* extension for players with prior lower-limb joint injuries. Healthy players get no turf penalty.
- **Congestion** has modest, well-measured effects (8); Dupont (9) is larger but a single club, so not used as a multiplier.
- **ACWR** is included with small weights only and flagged as contested (10-13).
- **Age**: conflicting (3 vs 2) -> attenuated design weight.
- Not found with usable soccer-specific numbers in this pass: ACL-specific multipliers, female-specific factors, exact re-injury time windows. Those are design choices or future work.
